Space Science Laboratory NavIC/GNSS Observational Campaign

Research Event Ongoing 2026

📍 Space Science Laboratory, SASL, VIT Bhopal University

Ongoing multi-frequency observational data collection investigating equatorial ionospheric perturbations, plasma bubble dynamics, and Total Electron Content (TEC) variations utilizing state-of-the-art dual-frequency NavIC/GNSS receiving systems. The dataset enables precise space weather forecasting and satellite communication impact assessments during Solar Cycle 25.
