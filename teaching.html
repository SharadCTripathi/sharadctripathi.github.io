Teaching Activities

Dr. Sharad Chandra Tripathi teaches undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral coursework in physics, computational physics, and space sciences. In particular, he teaches the prescribed Ph.D. Course Work course Solar – Terrestrial Physics (PHY6002) for Research Scholars, guiding scholars through advanced space weather mechanisms, solar transients, and ionospheric investigation techniques. His teaching integrates rigorous theoretical foundations with modern computational methods and observational datasets to prepare students and researchers for cutting-edge scientific investigation.

Solar – Terrestrial Physics

Ph.D. Course Work: Prescribed doctoral curriculum designed for Research Scholars in Physics and Space Sciences.
