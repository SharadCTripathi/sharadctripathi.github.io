Teaching Activities

Dr. Sharad Chandra Tripathi teaches undergraduate and postgraduate courses in physics, computational physics, and interdisciplinary scientific subjects. His teaching integrates theoretical concepts with computational tools, data analysis techniques, and laboratory experiments to provide students with strong analytical and research skills.
