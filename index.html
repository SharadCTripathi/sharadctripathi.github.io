About

Dr. Sharad Chandra Tripathi is a physicist and researcher specializing in Solar Physics, Space Weather, Ionospheric Physics, and Space Instrumentation. He earned his Ph.D. from Barkatullah Vishwavidyalaya, Bhopal, and was awarded the Erasmus Mundus INTACT Fellowship by the European Union. He completed post-doctoral research at Frederick University, Cyprus.

His research focuses on ionospheric plasma dynamics, solar flare effects, geomagnetic storms, and space weather phenomena using observational data and computational physics methods. He is the author of the academic monograph Space Weather: The Sun-Earth Perspectives (Scholar's Press, ISBN 978-3-639-76715-5) and has authored multiple peer-reviewed research publications in international journals.

In addition to research, Dr. Tripathi actively contributes to science communication and science popularization through physics articles, science blogs, and educational resources designed to make complex scientific ideas accessible to students and the general public.
